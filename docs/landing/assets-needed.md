# Revised Landing-Page Asset Requirements

## Ground rule

The supplied screenshots are research context only. They will not be cropped, sanitized, cleaned up, or used as production marketing assets. Purpose-built product visuals will be captured or created from the actual application only after the design and motion direction is approved.

## Required product states

| Product state needed | Why it is needed | Placement | What it communicates |
|---|---|---|---|
| One high-value keyword opportunity with demand, intent, difficulty, fit, and rationale | Makes prioritization inspectable | Hero motion and interactive product story | The system begins with evidence, not a prompt |
| The same opportunity selected and approved | Establishes a decision moment | Hero motion | The user remains in control today |
| The opportunity placed into a realistic editorial plan | Connects strategy to execution | Hero motion and product story | Research becomes a working plan |
| A context-rich content brief using audience, project memory, covered topics, research, and internal-link context | Proves differentiation from commodity generation | Product story | Each output carries shared project knowledge |
| A polished generated-asset preview with sources or research context | Demonstrates quality without pretending publication | Product story | The system prepares substantial content |
| Search-performance state for the same topic | Closes the loop | Measure and Improve section | Results are measured, not forgotten |
| Audit or decay signal paired with an improvement recommendation | Shows compounding behavior | Measure and Improve section | Performance creates the next action |
| A non-product conceptual continuation of the Growth Loop, labelled **Agent Mode — Coming Soon** | Makes the future direction tangible without inventing functionality | Agent Mode section | The connected workflow is the foundation for increasing automation |
| Project-context summary | Supports trust and continuity | Evidence section or motion layer | The stages share one memory and strategy |

All demo states should use one approved fictional or permissioned project so the narrative remains continuous.

## Motion-specific assets

- Approved product state for every stage in the selected concept.
- One demo keyword and topic that can credibly survive the full loop.
- Exact current/planned labels for each transition.
- Responsive layouts for desktop and mobile, not scaled screenshots.
- Static reduced-motion composition.
- Optional short poster frame for low-power devices and social previews.

## Brand assets required

- Vector logo and mark in light and dark variants.
- Approved brand color tokens and accessible contrast pairs.
- Display and body typeface decision with web licenses.
- Favicon and social-share image.
- Confirmation that the product icon system may be used in marketing compositions.
- Final brand voice guidance, if it exists.

## Confirmed commercial and proof assets

- Free plan exists and **Start Free** maps to a real offer.
- Pricing is confirmed at $0, $249/month, and $499/month with supplied limits.
- Real testimonials are available from Taggd and JaipurStuffs.
- Testimonial wording must remain exact unless the customer approves an edit.

## Human input still required

- Primary customer segment and buyer.
- Destination URL and account flow for **Start Free**.
- Whether the landing page should show pricing or link to a pricing page.
- Customer logo files and permission to display them.
- Whether reviewer names, roles, or headshots can be shown.
- Whether **Agent Mode — Coming Soon** should have a CTA and, if so, its real destination.
- Confirmation of current data sources and rights to display their logos.
- Exact current export formats and CMS roadmap language.
- Approved fictional or permissioned demo project and data.
- Substantiation or removal of the public 94%, 10x, 30+, and other quantitative claims.

## Purpose-built capture requirements

- No personal names, live domains, private project IDs, or customer data without permission.
- No empty, loading, warning, or failed-worker states unless a narrative explicitly requires them.
- Consistent viewport, density, typography, and data realism.
- Text remains readable at actual landing-page display size.
- Focus each state on one decision or transition.
- Do not depict interactions, automation, or publishing behavior that the application cannot perform.
- Agent Mode material must use the exact **Agent Mode — Coming Soon** label and must not resemble shipped product UI.

## Asset work explicitly deferred

- Capturing production screenshots.
- Building the hero composite.
- Creating the Agent Mode Coming Soon visual treatment.
- Producing the motion sequence.
- Editing the supplied research screenshots.

These begin only after a hero direction receives human approval and the implementation plan is accepted.
