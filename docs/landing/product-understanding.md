# Project Rankup: Product Understanding

## Research basis

This document is grounded in ten product screenshots supplied on 19 September 2026 and the public site at <https://projectrankup.com/>. The screenshots are treated as the source of truth for the current product. Public-site claims that are not demonstrated in those screenshots are marked planned or unverified.

## Product in one sentence

Project Rankup is building toward organic growth on autopilot: one system that researches opportunities, decides what matters, plans and creates content, measures results, and improves the next cycle.

Today, that vision is supported by a connected, human-directed workflow. **Agent Mode — Coming Soon** is the future layer intended to increasingly automate that workflow from start to finish.

## Primary user and job to be done

**Working assumption:** the primary buyer is a founder-led or lean B2B content team that needs consistent organic growth but does not have a full SEO operations team.

Their core job is not merely “write an AI article.” It is:

> Decide what is worth publishing, turn that decision into useful content, and keep improving what performs, with enough evidence and control to trust the process.

This audience assumption needs founder confirmation before final copy is approved. The current public page also gestures toward agencies and larger teams, which would materially change the proof, language, and CTA.

## Current product capabilities

### Project context

- Multiple projects, each tied to a domain.
- A project brief and persistent project memory used during generation.
- Memory of topics already covered to reduce repetition.
- Sitemap ingestion for internal-link opportunities.
- In-app and desktop notification settings.
- Usage allowances for content and AI credits.

### Keyword intelligence

- Organic keyword discovery with volume, difficulty, CPC, search intent, relevance, fit, and an AI score field.
- Competitor keyword benchmarking and coverage-gap discovery.
- Search Console-connected discovery.
- Per-keyword content-type selection.
- Direct actions to generate or schedule content.
- Bulk-selection and configurable table columns.

### Planning

- Editorial calendar with list and month-grid views.
- Manual keyword addition and generated keyword suggestions.
- Drag-to-reschedule behavior.
- Status counts for approved, scheduled, and awaiting-generation items.
- Direct transition from a scheduled item into generation.

### Content creation

- Separate creation studios for blog articles, ebooks, whitepapers, and LinkedIn posts.
- Blog brief fields for topic, primary keyword, audience, and semantic keywords.
- AI-assisted completion of empty brief fields.
- Tone and target-depth controls.
- Live research and project-context use are presented as part of generation.
- Review-before-generation step.

### Content management and improvement

- Searchable and filterable content history with title, keyword, status, word count, and scheduling actions.
- URL-based or uploaded-content audits.
- Whole-site scan for fast health checks.
- SEO, GEO, and AEO analysis are claimed in the audit UI.
- Enhanced regeneration is presented as an audit outcome.

### Measurement

- Search Console-style rank tracking for clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position.
- Date-range comparison and manual sync.
- An overview that surfaces scheduled work, generated assets, total keywords, and content-health actions.

## Planned or unverified capabilities

These must not be written as available without product confirmation:

- **Agent Mode — Coming Soon:** not implemented. Only the broad direction is established: it is intended to increasingly automate the organic-growth workflow from start to finish.
- Direct CMS publishing and automatic publishing. The current public FAQ says CMS links are on the roadmap and content is exported today.
- A fully automatic 30-day calendar that operates without review.
- Recurring, autonomous content audits and fixes.
- A mature SEO Copilot experience beyond the public-site mockup.
- Dedicated AI-citation or answer-engine visibility monitoring.
- Any implication that the system independently takes a site from zero to page one.

## The real workflow

1. Connect a domain and establish project context.
2. Discover organic and competitor keyword opportunities.
3. Evaluate relevance, fit, demand, difficulty, and intent.
4. Approve and place topics on the editorial calendar.
5. Generate a content brief and choose format, voice, and depth.
6. Review and create the asset.
7. Store, schedule, audit, and improve it.
8. Sync search performance and feed learning into the next decision.

The landing page should make this loop visible. It is the strongest credible differentiator in the current product.

## Positioning decision

The category is crowded with tools that own one of three stories: enterprise AI orchestration, content optimization, or fully autonomous publishing. Project Rankup should pursue the broader **Organic Growth on Autopilot** vision while making the maturity of each capability legible.

The positioning stack is:

1. **Outcome:** organic growth with less manual execution.
2. **System:** discover, prioritize, plan, create, measure, improve.
3. **Current proof:** a connected workflow with shared project context and explicit user actions.
4. **Coming Soon:** Agent Mode will increasingly automate that workflow.

“Autopilot” must describe the destination, not falsely imply that the current product autonomously publishes or guarantees growth. The landing page should visibly distinguish available now, partially assisted, and planned behavior.

## Product tensions to resolve

- The product supports an end-to-end loop, but its current navigation presents separate tools.
- The public page promises automation while the current workflow still relies on explicit user decisions.
- Project memory is strategically important but visually buried in Settings.
- “Scheduled” currently means scheduled in the planning workflow, not necessarily scheduled in a CMS.
- Four content formats demonstrate breadth, but the clearest acquisition wedge is SEO blog content.
- GEO/AEO language appears in the audit product, but the product does not yet visibly offer full AI-visibility tracking.

## Landing-page implication

Lead with the approved outcome: **Turn visibility into growth.** Immediately substantiate it with the connected workflow and current product states. Organic growth and the Growth Loop explain how the outcome is pursued. Present **Agent Mode — Coming Soon** as the strategic progression toward increasingly automated execution. Do not describe unfinalized Agent Mode jobs, schedules, triggers, permissions, approvals, or interface behavior.
