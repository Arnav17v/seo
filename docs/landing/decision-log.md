# Landing Page Decision Log

## Accepted decisions

- The final hero is approved: eyebrow “The organic-growth platform”; headline “Turn visibility into growth.”; supplied subheadline; CTAs “Start free” and “See how it works.”
- Visibility -> growth is the highest-level outcome. SEO, content, keyword research, organic growth, and the Growth Loop are mechanisms beneath it.
- “Visibility” intentionally supports future expansion into AI search, GEO/AEO, citations, recommendations, and other discovery surfaces without claiming those are all shipped today.
- Organic Growth on Autopilot is the product vision to explore at the highest positioning level.
- The connected workflow remains the proof system beneath that outcome, not the locked hero category.
- The product story is one loop: Discover -> Prioritize -> Plan -> Create -> Measure -> Improve.
- Agent Mode is strategically central but not implemented; it must always be labelled **Agent Mode — Coming Soon**.
- The approved narrative is: Today, the connected loop; Coming Soon, Agent Mode will increasingly automate it; Vision, Organic Growth on Autopilot.
- No detailed Agent Mode jobs, schedules, triggers, permissions, approvals, or UI may be invented before they are finalized.
- The Growth Loop is the selected major motion direction.
- Linear is the primary visual reference; Squarespace and Stripe are supporting references.
- Motion is a major quality signal, concentrated in one or two explanatory moments.
- The page should contain roughly seven major visual sections rather than translating every content point into its own section.
- Supplied screenshots are research evidence only, never production assets.
- Start Free is a valid primary CTA because the Free plan is confirmed.
- Confirmed pricing must influence perceived depth and polish.
- Taggd and JaipurStuffs testimonials are approved proof when reproduced faithfully.
- No implementation, `/ce-plan`, or `/ce-work` occurs before another human review.

## Rejected or demoted alternatives

- **“The connected SEO content system” as the locked top-level position:** demoted because it explains the mechanism but understates the intended product vision.
- **Unqualified “autopilot” claims today:** rejected because the current screenshots do not prove end-to-end autonomous operation or CMS publishing.
- **Fourteen large page sections:** rejected in favor of progressive product storytelling across roughly seven sections.
- **Squarespace as the primary visual reference:** demoted; its pacing remains useful, but Linear better fits the desired product-led SaaS character.
- **Using supplied screenshots in production:** rejected; purpose-built application states will be created later.
- **Expanding this assignment into product IA design:** rejected; only UX findings that affect landing-page truth and communication remain in scope.
- **Animating every section:** rejected; motion impact depends on restraint.
